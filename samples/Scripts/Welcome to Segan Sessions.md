# Welcome to Segan Sessions

Hi! This is your teleprompter.

Press the space bar to start scrolling. Press it again to pause.

Drag this card by its top bar and park it right under your camera, so your eyes stay close to the lens.

To read your own script, drop any .md or .txt file into the Scripts folder: Movies, then Segan Sessions, then Scripts. It shows up in this list the next time you open it.

Now press Record and read this out loud. That's your first take.

Short sentences read best. One idea per line.

And smile. You're on camera.
